A demo. Each card pools a pitcher's regular-season game cards: IP, ERA, WHIP, strikeouts, K% and BB% are MLB's season line; CSW% comes from his pitches. The Stuff, Locations and PLV letters are his season means on season cuts, fitted to the same distribution as the game letters. Movement shows each pitch type's KDE in three tiers, the regions holding its densest 95%, 63% and 32% of pitches. The two location plots show 100 pitches between them, each side's pitch types by their share of all the pitches (30% four-seamers to righties, 30 of them in the vs RHB plot; at least one of each type thrown to a side), drawn at random on one fixed seed.
