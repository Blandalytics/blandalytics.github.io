Pick a date, a game and a pitcher.
PLV and Location are provisional below MLB (the models aren't yet calibrated to minor-league strike zones and parks).
Data (especially pitch types) are subject to change. Cards are built each morning for the previous day's completed games at every level with Statcast tracking (MLB, Triple-A, the Florida State League's Hawk-Eye parks, the Arizona Fall League, ...), back to the start of the season; while MLB games are on, a Live entry carries cards for the pitchers in them, updated within a minute of each pitch. Please send any feedback to Kyle Bland, on Bluesky or Twitter.
