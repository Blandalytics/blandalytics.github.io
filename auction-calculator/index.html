Each player is scored against the pool (z-scores per category, with rate stats weighted by playing time, or points), less replacement level. By lineup slot, the league's hitter and pitcher slots are filled with the most total value; a position's replacement level is the average value of the slots around its cutoff (the last few starters there and the first few left over), so its scarcity comes from the projections, not a fixed order, and a player is priced at the scarcest position he plays. Exactly the players who fill every team's legal roster (lineups, plus the bench unless it's minimized) are drafted: the last of them goes for the min bid, the dollars above the min bids are split between hitters and pitchers and handed out by value over him, and they add up to the league's budget.
