How It Works

Standings Points

One point in a category is how far apart team totals sit when every team takes one player from each tier of the rostered pool.

Rate Stats

AVG, ERA and WHIP count as gains over the rostered-player rate, weighted by at-bats or innings, so a great rate in a small sample is worth less.

Replacement Level

The average of the best three players left once every starting and bench spot is filled. A position gets its own lower level only when no one eligible there is starting elsewhere.

Dollars

Hitters and pitchers each keep the minimum bid for every roster spot and split the rest by points above replacement. Every period sums to the full budget.

Eligibility

10 games or 5 starts at a position that season. SP takes 3 starts, RP 5 relief outings. Ohtani's hitting and pitching are two players, as Yahoo lists them.

Head-to-Head Leagues

Values use roto standings. Players' values relative to each other change little under head-to-head.
