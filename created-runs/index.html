Reading the table

Every number is created runs above an RE24 baseline built from the 2026 season. The table covers the 205 hitters with at least 400 PA. Pick any column heading to rank them by it, highest first; pick it again for lowest first. It shows 20 hitters at a time, so scroll it for the rest. Values shade from white at zero toward red at the highest value among these hitters and blue at the lowest.

Created Runs

CR
Total created runs, batting plus running.
CR/600
CR scaled to 600 plate appearances.

Batting

bCR
Created runs at the plate: every column in this group added together.
State Change
Expected runs from each ball in play, given the base-out state, exit velocity, launch angle and spray angle.
Hit Luck
How much more or less a line drive, fly ball or popup was worth than its contact deserved: a blooper falling in, or a hard liner caught.
BB, HBP, K
Runs from walks (intentional included), hit-by-pitches and strikeouts.

Running

rCR
Created runs on the bases: every column in this group added together.
Home-to-First
How much more or less a ground ball was worth than its contact deserved: beating out the throw, or being thrown out on a grounder that should have been a hit.
BsR
Extra bases taken or given away on balls in play, against the average runner from the same spot.
SB
Runs from stolen bases.
CS+PO
Runs lost to times caught stealing and pickoffs, together.
