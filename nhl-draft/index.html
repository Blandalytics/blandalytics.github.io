NHL Draft Tool

Sit in one seat of a 12-team roto snake draft. Every option is priced by simulating the rest of the draft, and shown as the team it leads to — roto points, category by category. Runs entirely in your browser.

Settings

Do not draft

Kept off your options and board. Rivals may still take them, and you can still pick one by name.

Commands

Take an option with its button or number. p name takes any available player (do-not-draft included, and at a rival's stop this records their pick). b RW 25 lists the best RW-eligible players on your board. s re-runs the simulations on fresh draws, u rewinds one stop.
