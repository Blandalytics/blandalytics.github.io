How it works

Scale: 100 is the average park at that level. Park Factor is the wOBA index and R is runs (BaseRuns). The other columns are indexes for each outcome: BACON is Batting Average on CONtact (home runs included), wOBACon is wOBA on Contact, and HR p10 / p90 factors provide an 80% Confidence Interval for the park's HR true factor. The spread is wider for newer parks or those with recent renovations (Ex: Kauffman Stadium in 2026).

Colors: centered on 100 and saturated at 90 and 110. Red helps hitters and blue hurts them, so for K the scale is flipped: more strikeouts shade blue.

Model: one joint Bayesian fit of every plate appearance (MLB 2015–26; AAA, AA, A+ and A 2021–26) with batter, pitcher, league-season, home-field and platoon terms, a per-venue factor that drifts across seasons (with breaks for known dimension changes) and a per-venue left/right split. If a park is used across multiple leagues (Ex: Sutter Health Park has hosted both AAA and MLB games), the data from all leagues informs that park factor.

Games: MLB 2027 rows use the 2027 schedule (games at the venue that season, neutral sites included); every other year counts the venue's completed regular-season games that season. Qualified parks hides venues with 50 or fewer games, pro-rated to each level's home schedule, so shortened seasons scale down: . PA is the venue's plate appearances at that level in the data: that season's for past years, and every season's for each level's most recent year.

Seasonal: MLB 2027 parks week by week, from a second fit of the same model with two curves by date added: the league's own calendar (every park together: cold April air, summer heat, September call-ups) and each park's deviation from it (a curve for its roof type plus its own). Drag the slider or press ▶ to move through the 2027 season. Compared to Season average sets 100 at the average park over the whole season, so the factors carry the league's calendar too (home runs run about 8% below average in early April): how the park plays that week. Same week sets 100 at that week's average park, which leaves only the park's own pattern (roofed parks such as American Family Field play relatively better in April, before the summer lift elsewhere). The MLB average row is the league that week against its season average. Most parks move only a point or two over the season, home runs the most, so this view shows one decimal. A park's seasonal factors average out to about its Full season factor (the two fits can differ by a point).

All levels: every row is still indexed to its own level's average, so a 105 in AA and a 105 in MLB are each 5% above their level, not equal environments. Picking an Org switches to All levels to show the whole system; pick a level after that to narrow it.

Years: each level opens on its most recent year: 2027 for MLB, built on the 2027 schedule, and 2026 for the minors. Earlier seasons (MLB from 2015, the minors from 2021) come from the same fit, indexed to that season's average park at that level, for all batters; the LHB/RHB split covers each level's most recent year. Venue names, teams, leagues and orgs are as they were that season, from MLB's StatsAPI, with 2021's interim minor-league names shown as the leagues' current ones (Triple-A East as IL, and so on). All years lists every season at once, one row per park and season; with a Team or Org picked, it follows a park through the years.
